About Me

EE Student & Independent AI Builder

Hi, I'm Muhammad Ibrahim Zia, a first-year Electrical Engineering student at NED University of Engineering & Technology (NED UET) in Karachi, and an independent builder of AI tools.

I build through AI-assisted development: I research a problem properly, direct AI tools to implement it, then test, break and refine the result. My hand-written Python is basic, and I'd rather say so plainly than pretend otherwise. What I bring is choosing problems worth solving, designing the system, and shipping something that actually runs.

My work so far centres on running AI locally and applying ML to real data: an offline runner for Microsoft's experimental BitNet models, an exoplanet classifier built for the NASA Space Apps Challenge, and experiments with quantized local models and retrieval-augmented generation.

On the engineering side I'm drawn to energy and space problems. I'm an IEEE Student Member (PES, OES and IES societies) and I'm currently exploring a degradation-aware adaptive MPPT approach for CubeSat solar arrays, which is early-stage work rather than a finished project.
